# 0010. Choose what to track at onboarding

- **Status:** Superseded by [0011](0011-four-tabs-always.md)
- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Links:** `docs/design/training/OnboardingMode.dc.html` (historical)

## Context
Adding Training risked cluttering the app for people who only track food, and the reverse.

## Decision
Onboarding asked "Track nutrition, training, or both" and hid tabs to match. The choice was device-only and cleared on sign-out.

## Alternatives considered
- Always show everything (later chosen, see 0011).

## Consequences
Hidden tabs made features hard to discover, complicated home routing and Settings (the weight unit had to stay reachable in every mode), and the setting was removed the same week.
